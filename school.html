Item 1: Sales Promotion & Fundraising

A manager of a company organised a sales promotion in several market centres and a fundraising event to support community projects.

During the fundraising, the Board of Directors contributed Shs12,000,000. The company staff contributed half the amount contributed by the Board, while the sales promotion team contributed 0.05 of the increased sales.

The total funds collected were donated to community schools and charity organisations in the ratio 3:5, respectively.

Task:

  1. Determine the greatest number of equal teams that could be formed.
  2. Find the number of female and male workers in each team.
  3. Determine the number of market centres visited.
  4. Calculate the amount by which the company's sales increased.
  5. Determine the total amount donated to: (i) community schools; (ii) charity organisations.

  1. the original area of the garden
  2. the number of metres by which each dimension should be increased
  3. the new base and height.

(b) Determine:

  1. the cost of one kilogram of seeds

(c) Calculate the number of kilograms of seeds the neighbour can buy with Shs580,000.
